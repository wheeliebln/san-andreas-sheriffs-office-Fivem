Our Mission

The San Andreas Sheriff’s Office is responsible for providing law enforcement, corrections security, and emergency services to the communities of San Andreas. Every day, our Officers and staff work to protect the public, enforce the law, and respond to incidents that threaten the safety and order of the county.

We operate across a wide range of duties. General Duties handle calls for service, Highway Patrol, and criminal investigations. Corrections staff maintain secure custody of inmates and ensure the safe operation of detention facilities. Specialized units support emergency response, community outreach, and critical incident management when situations escalate beyond routine calls. Our commitment is straightforward: deliver professional, consistent, and accountable service. We uphold the law without favor, treat the public with respect, and hold ourselves to high standards of conduct. Whether responding to a violent crime, securing a detention facility, or assisting a community in need, the San Andreas Sheriff’s Office stands ready. We exist to serve the people of San Andreas, maintain order, and protect those who live and work within our county.
